Mini-pig:

a dwarf pig in your home

Getting a mini pig is not just a trendy choice, but a big responsibility

  • Size

    The height at the withers is up to 50 cm, but much depends on the pig's diet.

  • Scale

    Mini-pigs come in various breeds, and depending on this, their maximum weight ranges from 8-12 kg to 32-68 kg.

  • Coloration

    They can be sandy, red, black, marble, tiger, or mixed colors. There are also individuals with neat spots.

Conditions of detention

Just like regular pigs, miniature pigs are prone to gaining excess weight. They require a diet and control over all the food they consume. Young piglets are fed 3-4 times a day, while adults are fed in the morning and evening, twice a day.
Mini pigs love:
• vegetables: carrots, pumpkin, zucchini, Jerusalem artichoke, turnip, pattypan squash (raw or boiled);
• fruits: pears, apples, grapes, bananas;
• grains: rice, millet, buckwheat, barley;
• fermented dairy products: sour milk, kefir, cheese, whey.

After acquiring a squeaky pet, it is essential to vaccinate it against parvovirus, swine fever, erysipelas, and rabies. Anti-parasitic treatment is also important and should be carried out once every three months (for helminths). If you notice strange behavior in the animal or unusual symptoms, hurry to the veterinarian. It is important to know that some diseases of mini pigs can also be transmitted to humans (for example, mycoplasmosis, ascariasis, salmonellosis, chlamydiosis, etc.).Be sure to follow personal hygiene rules and everything will be fine!

Mini pigs need their hooves trimmed on time. Regular walks on asphalt paths help naturally wear down the piglet's hooves, and the more often you walk, the less frequently they will need trimming.
Water procedures for the piglet should be taken no more than once every 2 weeks. The piglets really enjoy splashing in the water and don’t mind bathing at all.

Mini pig breeds

Gottinngen Mini Pig

The Göttingen Minipig is a breed of domestic pig developed in the 1960s in Germany by specialists at the University of Göttingen. Vietnamese Pot-bellied pigs, Minnesota Minipigs, and German Landrace pigs were used in its development.

Siberian Mini Pig

These attractive animals differ from their fellow pigs in that they lack a belly and have a rather defined and toned body, despite their ancestors being pot-bellied pigs.Siberian pigs reach a maximum weight of 50 kg.

Read more

Wiesenau Mini Pig

Wiesenau are compact mini-pigs weighing 20-25 kg. These pigs were bred by German breeders. A distinctive feature of Wiesenau pigs is their coat color – black with white spots or, conversely, white with black spots. Their faces are virtually devoid of wrinkles.

Read more

Bergstresser squeaks

Bergstrasse Knirt miniature pigs can be white or red, with black spots permitted. Another color option is black with white spots. These dwarf pigs weigh no more than 7-15 kg.

Read more

Mini-maialino

The smallest and youngest mini-pig breed today is the Mini-Maialino, originally from Italy. Adults weigh a maximum of 10-12 kg. It's no wonder this is one of the most expensive mini-pig breeds.

Read more

Vietnamese pot-bellied pig

This mini-pig breed weighs between 45 and 100 kg. Its distinctive feature is reflected in its name: a drooping belly. Black is the predominant color, though marbled or white are rarely seen.
